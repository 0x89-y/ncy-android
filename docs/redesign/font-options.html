Font options

The same ink-blue screens with six type pairings: one for titles and names, one for notes and the interface, and a mono for times and labels. The second row of buttons still switches the accent.

Dark mode uses a dark grey tinted toward the accent (never near-black) with text at about 12:1. Your notes sit on the accent tint and other devices' on plain paper. The selected filter, the Up next card, the mic and New chat all use the accent.
