Ncy redesign, round 1

Two directions for the Android app, four screens each: home, a chat, the long-press actions, and settings. The sample data covers every message type the app has: text, checklist, voice with transcript, photo, file, reply, reaction, reminder and synced notes.

Idea

Ncy as a notebook rather than a messenger. Notes run down the page like journal entries, with no bubbles. It's calm and good for reading back, and keeps the "chat" speed for writing.

Type

Newsreader (serif) for names and titles, Instrument Sans for UI and notes, JetBrains Mono for times and labels.

Colour

Warm paper with black ink, plus one terracotta accent. Chats get muted tints, never loud fills.

Navigation

One home screen. Filters are visible chips (All, Starred, Reminders, Archived) instead of a hidden filter menu. Settings sits in the top bar.

Idea

Ncy as a personal messenger: friendly, colourful and fast. Each chat has its own colour, used for its avatar, its pinned card and its bubbles, so you know where you are at a glance.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Geist for UI and messages. Rounder and more playful than A.

Colour

Cool neutral base with an indigo accent. Six chat colours, each dark enough for white text (4.5:1 or better).

Navigation

Bottom bar with Chats, Starred, Reminders and Settings, so starred notes and reminders get real homes. Pinned chats become cards with checklist progress.
